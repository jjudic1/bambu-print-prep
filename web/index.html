Handoff3D

3D print… no computer necessary

Prepare a 3D model for your Bambu Lab printer on an iPad. Drop in any model — STL, 3MF, OBJ or PLY — and get one your printer will take. Resize it to an exact measurement, flatten a curved bottom so it stands on the plate, and if it is too big for your bed, split it and spread it over as many plates as it needs.

Works with every current Bambu Lab machine — A1 mini, A1, P1P, P1S, P2S, X1, X1 Carbon, X1E, X2D, A2L, H2C, H2S, H2D and H2D Pro — in every nozzle size, with the bed and the print settings taken from Bambu Studio's own profiles. Out comes a Bambu project file you upload to MakerWorld and print from Bambu Handy.

There is no version of Bambu Studio for iPad. This runs in Safari, costs nothing, needs no account, and does all of the work on your device — your model is never uploaded.
